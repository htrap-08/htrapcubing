Read the language of the cube.

Every algorithm is a sentence of turns. Start with a face, then add direction, width, slices, or rotations.

RTurn the right face clockwise
Clockwise is always from the viewpoint of the face you are turning. The apostrophe (′) reverses that direction; a 2 means a 180° turn.
